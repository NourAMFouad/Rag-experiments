1. What is .gitignore? Why do we need it?
🔹 Purpose:
The .gitignore file tells Git which files/folders NOT to track or push to GitHub.

🔹 Why?
You often have files you don’t want to share, like:

Local environments (.venv/)

Logs (*.log)

OS/Editor files (.DS_Store, *.swp)

Machine learning model files (*.h5, *.pt, *.pkl)

Notebooks with big outputs

Without .gitignore, those files will clutter your repo or even expose sensitive data.
